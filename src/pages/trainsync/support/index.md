Need help with Train&Sync? Email [salahy.allaithy@gmail.com](mailto:salahy.allaithy@gmail.com). Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please leave out passwords, personal photos, and full backups.

- **Find a saved workout:** Open **My log** and select the session.
- **Keep a copy of your data:** Open **My log → Log tools** and save a backup to a location you control.
- **A linked exercise image won’t load:** Check the image URL and your connection, then check **Download link images** in **Privacy and data**.
- **Privacy questions:** Read the **Privacy Policy** or contact support.
