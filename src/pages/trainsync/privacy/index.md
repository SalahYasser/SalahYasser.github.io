**Effective date:** 2026-11-13

Train&Sync stores your workout plans, completed sessions, preferences, and personal exercise photos on your device. The app does not require a Train&Sync account. Device backups may include app data depending on your system settings.

When you download an exercise video, the app contacts its video host, including Supabase for hosted videos. If downloads for linked images are enabled, displaying an image from a link can contact its host. These requests expose connection information such as your IP address to the relevant host. Your workout history and personal photos are not sent as part of these requests. You can stop new linked-image requests and clear that cache in Privacy & Data, and remove downloaded videos separately.

You choose where to export or share files. A plan file includes the plan structure, but excludes personal weights, workout history, and photos. A backup can contain saved data and personal photos and is not encrypted by the app. Your chosen file destination may sync the export. Deleting a local item does not delete copies you have exported or copies retained in device backups.

Photo selection, camera use, and notifications are optional. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. When you visit these pages, GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. If you email support, we use your address and the information you choose to send to respond and investigate the issue.
